Help

If you have a question or a problem, please email us.

Contact hello@shallwedog.com

We reply within 3 business days. Requests to see or delete your personal information are answered within 10 days after we confirm your identity.

To help us help you faster, tell us your device (iPhone or Android) and app version. For payment questions, please include the order number on your store receipt.

Custom dog orders

How long does it take?

We usually deliver your dog in the app within 48 hours, and at the latest within 7 days of payment. Before you order, the screen shows when to expect it, such as “If you order now, you’ll get it within 48 hours”.

When there are many orders, it may take a few more days. A person checks every finished dog before we send it, between 10 a.m. and 2 a.m. (Korea time), so orders placed after 2 a.m. are checked from 10 a.m. We send a notification when your dog is ready. If it is finished between 9 pm and 8 am, the notification comes at 8 am (your phone’s time).

It doesn’t look like the photos

We fix it once for free within 14 days of receiving it. In the app, go to My orders, tap Ask for changes, choose what looks different (face, body, color, fur, other) and add a short note.

Your dog is made in the game’s style, so it may not look exactly like the photos.

Can I get a refund?

A custom dog is made to order, so under the Korean E-Commerce Consumer Protection Act it cannot be withdrawn or refunded. We tell you this and ask for your agreement before payment.

However, we refund you if we cannot deliver within 7 days or cannot make it for reasons on our side. If you feel it doesn’t look like your dog, we fix it once for free instead of a refund, as above.

For other paid items, you can withdraw within 7 days from the later of the purchase date and the date the item became available. Items that are used up right away, such as paws already spent or styling already applied, cannot be withdrawn. See Sections 9 and 10 of the Terms.

Refunds go through the store you paid with: App Store at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google Play via the Google Play refund page. Each store’s policies also apply.

What happens to my photos?

We use your photos only to make and fix your custom dog, and delete them automatically 14 days after the last delivery. Location data is removed from photos before they are sent.

Only if you separately agreed to “keep photos for 1 year” when ordering do we keep them for one year; tap Settings > Withdraw photo keeping to delete them right away.

To make your dog in 3D, your photos are sent to outside processing companies (OpenAI, Anthropic, Meshy; overseas). See Section 5 of the Privacy Policy.

Can I order two dogs together?

Yes. Choose the two-dog product to order two dogs with one payment. Add two photos and an order form for each dog. Each dog is sent to the app as soon as it is ready.

It says “We’re checking your payment”

We start making your dog as soon as the store confirms your payment. If confirmation is delayed, we check again every hour. If it still shows this after 48 hours, please email hello@shallwedog.com.

It says “We can’t take more orders today”

We accept up to 3 custom orders per account per day (24 hours). A two-dog order counts as 2. If you reach the limit before paying, the app tells you “You can order up to 3 times a day”. If an order you already paid for goes over the limit, we don’t cancel the payment; we continue making it the next day.

Each photo must be under 5 MB, and the one-line note can be up to 60 characters.

You asked me to send new photos

If a photo is blurry or the face is not from the front, or if it is not a photo of a dog, is inappropriate or is too small, we may ask for new photos. You can send new photos once from My orders > Send photos again. If there is still a problem, we will email you.

How do I sign in?

There is no sign-in button. If you check “I am 14 or older” when you first start, the app connects automatically with Game Center on iPhone or Google Play Games on Android. If you do not check it, you can still play everything, and your progress is saved only on your phone. You can turn it on later in Settings > Account save.

Account save and custom orders need this connection. If the window does not appear, sign in at phone Settings > Game Center on iPhone, or in the Google Play Games app on Android, then open the app again.

Choosing good photos

These are the same tips as on the order screen in the app. Please send two photos.

  • 🐶First: face from the front
    Make sure the face is clearly visible from the front.
  • 🐕Second: whole body
    Show the whole body while standing.
  • 🔍Not blurry
    Choose a photo that is in focus.
  • ☝️Only one dog
    Make sure only one dog is in the photo.
  • 🙈No human faces
    Choose a photo without people’s faces.

Tips for a closer likeness: photos taken in good light, without filters or edits, work best. Don’t mix photos with different fur, such as before and after grooming; send two photos from the same time as the “Coat now” you chose in the order form.

Account and data

I changed phones

If the new phone is the same kind (iPhone to iPhone, Android to Android), sign in with the same Game Center or Google Play Games account and you pick up where you left off (when account save is on; see the steps). If you switched between iPhone and Android, email hello@shallwedog.com and we will send your custom dog to the new phone again. Items bought in a store cannot be moved to the other store under the store rules.

I changed phones and can’t see what I bought

Tap Settings > Restore purchases in the app. If you are signed in to the same store account (Apple ID or Google account) you used to buy, items you buy once and keep, such as the Full version, are turned back on.

Items that are used up, such as paws, are not restored this way; if account save is on, they come back with account save as described below. Custom dogs you received can be downloaded again when you are connected with the same Game Center or Google Play Games account. If you still can’t see them, email us with the order number on your store receipt.

I want to move my progress to a new phone

  1. On your current phone, check that Settings > Account save is on. If it is off, check “I am 14 or older” and turn it on.
  2. On the new phone, sign in with the same Game Center (iPhone) or Google Play Games (Android) account, then open the app.
  3. Your paws (free and paid), purchases, dog list and game progress come back. Photos are not saved.

If the new phone already has other progress, a screen asks which one to use. Progress does not move between iPhone and Android.

If you are under 14 or did not turn on account save, your progress is only on your phone and does not move. The Full version and decorations bought with money come back with Restore purchases above, and you can ask for a refund of paid paws that are left, as below.

I want a refund for paid paws

Within 7 days of buying, you can withdraw from a purchase of paid paws you have not used, for the full amount. After 7 days, we refund the paid paws that are left when you stop using the Service and delete your account and data (the price divided by the number of paws, minus a 10% fee). Paws already spent and decorations bought with paws are not refunded. Free paws are spent first.

Email hello@shallwedog.com with your device (iPhone or Android), your Game Center or Google Play Games nickname and the order number on your store receipt. We send the money to your bank account ourselves; we ask for the account details in our reply and delete them right after sending. If account save is on, we can check your remaining paid paws. If not, please also send a screenshot of your paws in the app. See Section 8 of the Terms for details.

My child checked “I am 14 or older”

As the parent or legal guardian, please email hello@shallwedog.com. Tell us the device and your child’s Game Center or Google Play Games nickname. Once we confirm it, we delete that account’s saved progress and account information and let you know by email. Progress on the phone stays, so your child can keep playing.

If anything was bought without your consent, please mention it in the same email (Section 12 of the Terms).
