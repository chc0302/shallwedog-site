Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026 · 한국어

About this translation. The Korean version of this policy is the original. This English version is a reference translation. If the two differ, the Korean version applies, except where the law of your country requires otherwise.

K-Puff Lab (“we”, “us”) runs the Shall We Dog? app and the website shallwedog.com (the “Service”). This policy explains what personal information we process, why, for how long, and who helps us process it.

At a glance

  • Your game progress is stored on your phone. Only users who check “I am 14 or older” when they first start are connected to our servers, for account save (moving to a new phone) and custom orders. If you do not check it, nothing is sent to our account save or order servers. Before you check it, no usage statistics or crash reports are sent either.
  • There is no sign-in button. The connection is made automatically with Game Center on iPhone or Google Play Games on Android. If it does not happen, you can still play.
  • Photos sent for a custom dog order are deleted automatically 14 days after the last delivery.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we never delete your data on our own initiative.
  • You can delete your data at any time with Request data deletion at the bottom of the app’s Settings, or by email.

3. Children under 14

Anyone can play. When you first start, you can choose “I am 14 or older” (optional; we do not ask for a date of birth). Only users who check it are connected to our servers for account save and custom orders. If you do not check it, your progress stays only on your phone and nothing is sent to our account save or order servers; before you check it and while it is unchecked, no usage statistics (Firebase Analytics) or crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) are sent (they are off by default and turn on when you check it), and we ask Google to show only non-personalised ads. Once you are 14 or older, you can turn it on in the app’s Settings.

If we learn that a child under 14 checked it, or a legal guardian tells us at hello@shallwedog.com, we promptly delete that account’s saved progress, account information and orders. Only the transaction records we must keep by law are kept separately as described in Section 2.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or give personal information to other companies, except when authorities request it through procedures set by law.

6. Deletion

When a retention period ends or you ask us to delete, we delete within 5 days. Electronic files are deleted so they cannot be recovered; we do not keep personal information on paper. Information we must keep by law is stored separately and used only for that purpose. Copies in backups for recovery are deleted automatically after up to 30 days, as described in Section 2.

7. Your rights

  • You can ask to see, correct, delete or stop the processing of your information, and withdraw consent, at any time.
  • Tapping Request data deletion at the bottom of the app’s Settings deletes your server data (account information, account save records, custom dog re-downloads, order and production records, notification tokens) and your server sign-in account (Firebase). The next time you open the app, it is disconnected from our servers and only the records on your phone remain. Paid paws left in your account save are deleted too, so you can ask for a refund first (Terms, Section 8); copies in backups for recovery are deleted after up to 30 days. Data on your device remains and is removed when you delete the app. See Account & data deletion.
  • For other requests, email hello@shallwedog.com. After confirming your identity we reply within 10 days. A legal guardian or authorised representative may also make requests.
  • Transaction records we must keep by law (Section 2) are kept separately for that period even if you delete your account, and then deleted.

8. Advertising and analytics

To keep the game free, we show ads through Google LLC’s AdMob. Without the Full Version you see banner ads, interstitial ads after an activity ends and rewarded ads you choose to watch; with the Full Version there are only ads for continuing a mini game (Terms, Section 5). Google uses device information, in-app activity and an approximate location inferred from your IP address to show ads and measure them.

  • iPhone: we do not ask for permission to track, and we do not show personalised ads that combine information from other apps or websites. Only non-personalised ads are shown.
  • EU and UK: before showing ads, we display a Google-certified consent message. If you do not consent, only limited ads that use less personal information are shown. You can change your choice in Settings at any time.
  • Android: you can delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads (menu names vary by device).

We use Google LLC’s Firebase for usage statistics and crash reports. We do not send names or contact details that identify you. Before you check “I am 14 or older”, and if you do not check it, no usage statistics or crash reports are sent (Section 3).

9. Notifications

  • Order notices (custom dog ready, fixed dog ready, new photos needed, refund) are sent to the person who ordered. We ask once, after you send your first custom order, whether you want them. They are never sent between 9 pm and 8 am on your phone’s clock; anything due in that time is sent at 8 am.
  • Play reminders are sent once a day at 8 pm, plus once at 6 pm if you have not given food or water that day. You get them only if you chose the evening reminder on the first start screen and allowed notifications on your phone; you can turn them on or off in the app’s Settings. They are scheduled on your phone and nothing is sent to our servers. They are never sent between 9 pm and 8 am.
  • Notifications never contain advertising such as discounts, prices or sales, so we do not ask for separate consent to marketing messages.
  • You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone’s notification settings.

10. Security

  • Only the operator can access personal information.
  • Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Server storage rules let each user read only their own data.
  • Work computers are password-locked, and service access keys are stored separately from code.
