1. Purpose

2. Definitions

  • User: anyone who agrees to these terms and uses the Service.
  • Account: user identification we create by connecting the Game Center (iPhone) or Google Play Games (Android) account of a user who checked “I am 14 or older” to our servers.

  • Paws: the in-game currency. We keep paid paws (bought with money) separate from free paws (earned in the game).
  • Paid products: things you buy with money, such as the Full Version, extra dog slots (Dog slot +1, Dog slot +3), decorations sold for money (seasonal sets and single items — Winter season set, Santa hat, Reindeer antlers, Christmas bed, Christmas Tree; 4 jewel necklaces; My photo frame for your home wall), paid paws (700 Paws, etc.) and Custom Dogs. Basic decorations are bought with paws.
  • Full Version: a bundle of paid features that you keep once bought. It includes 3 dog slots (including your first dog) and Full Version decorations, shows no banner or interstitial ads, and gives you rewards right away without watching an ad. Continuing a mini game (such as “Watch an ad to continue” in Hurdle Run) still means watching an ad, even with the Full Version.
  • Custom Dog: a 3D dog we make individually from the photos and order form you send, and deliver to you.

3. Posting and changing these terms

These terms are posted on this website, and you can open them from the line at the bottom of the app’s Settings. We may change them within the law. When we change them, we explain what is changing and why through an in-app notice and on this page. If you do not agree with the changes, you may stop using the Service and delete your account.

If the new phone already has other progress, a screen asks which one to use. Progress does not move between iPhone and Android.

Without the Full Version, the app shows ads: banner ads at the bottom of the home and walk screens, interstitial ads right before the result screen of a walk, mini game, agility run or training (never before competitions or award ceremonies; at most once every 5 minutes, and none in the first 24 hours after you install), and ads you can choose to watch for a reward. The Full Version has no banner or interstitial ads, and rewards come right away without an ad. Continuing a mini game (such as “Watch an ad to continue” in Hurdle Run) still means watching an ad, even with the Full Version.

  • Each photo must be under 5 MB, and the free-text line can be up to 60 characters.
  • We accept up to 3 custom orders per account per day (24 hours). Orders over the limit are not canceled; we continue making them the next day.
  • We start making your dog after the store confirms your payment. If confirmation is delayed, we check again every hour (for up to 48 hours); if it is still not confirmed, please email us.

  • However, we refund you if we fail to deliver within 7 days or cannot make it for reasons on our side.
  • A person checks each Custom Dog before delivery, and we do not make changes after delivery.
  • A Custom Dog may differ from the real dog. We make it within what a 3D dog in the game can express.
  • Finished Custom Dogs are delivered only to the ordering user’s account, and we do not publish them elsewhere, such as on our website or store pages (except as below if you choose “Examples”). However, only when you yourself enter online features such as the plaza or matches, other users in the same place see your dog’s in-game look (including a Custom Dog) while you are there. If you do not enter them, it is not shown (Section 13). You can download them again until you delete your account.
  • Examples (optional): only if you check “You may show my finished in-game dog as an example” on the order form, we show how your finished dog looks in the game as an example on our website, in the app and on our store pages. We never use the photos you sent or your dog’s name. The box is not checked in advance, and you can order without checking it. We take it down any time you ask at hello@shallwedog.com.
