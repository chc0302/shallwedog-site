Show Us Your Dog — Official Rules

Full rules for the Shall We Dog? launch event on Instagram (@shallwedog).

  1. Dates: November 15 – November 21, 2026, 11:59 PM KST. Comments outside these dates are not counted.
  2. How to enter: Post a photo of a dog you live with (or once shared your life with) in the comments of the event post. Following, sharing or tagging friends is not required.
  3. Gifts: The photo with the most likes gets a free custom 3D character that looks like that dog (Full Version included; custom only if you already have the Full Version). 10 random entries get 3,800 Paws in the game.
  4. One entry per person: One photo comment per account. If you post more than one, only the first counts. If one person enters with several accounts, all of them are removed.
  5. Photos: Only photos you took or have the right to post. Photos of other people's dogs, reposted photos and photos with large human faces are removed.
  6. Most likes: Decided by the like count at the end of the event. Comments with bought likes, bots or like-for-like groups are removed and the next comment moves up. If likes are tied, the earlier comment wins.
  7. Random picks: 10 entries are drawn at random from all entries except the most-liked one. The draw is screen-recorded and can be shown on request.
  8. Winners: Announced on November 24, 2026 in the @shallwedog Story and a pinned comment on the event post.
  9. Contact: Winners get a DM from @shallwedog only. We never ask for passwords, card numbers or money by DM. Please ignore messages from look-alike accounts. If we get no reply within 7 days of the announcement, the gift goes to the next entry.
  10. Receiving gifts: Send us your in-app [user ID — to be filled after tab 4 answers] by DM and we'll add the gift to that account. Photos for the custom character are sent through the in-app custom order screen (same photo storage and deletion rules as a normal order). Gifts can't be transferred or exchanged for cash.
  11. Use of photos: By entering, you agree that we may use your photo and username, with credit, in the winner announcement and in Shall We Dog? Instagram posts. We will ask you separately before using it in paid ads. If you'd like us to stop, DM us or email hello@shallwedog.com and we'll take it down right away.
  12. Age: You must be 14 or older to enter.
  13. Instagram: This promotion is in no way sponsored, endorsed or administered by, or associated with, Instagram. By entering, you release Instagram from all liability. Information you give for this promotion is given to Shall We Dog?, not to Instagram.
  14. Changes: If we can't give a gift for reasons on the app's side, we'll give another in-app item of the same value and post the change on the event post.

Personal information is handled under our Privacy Policy. Questions: hello@shallwedog.com
