1. Purpose

These terms set out the conditions and procedures for using the mobile game Shall We Dog? and related services (the “Service”) provided by K-Puff Lab (“we”, “us”), and the rights and duties of us and our users.

2. Definitions

  • User: anyone who agrees to these terms and uses the Service.
  • Account: user identification we create by connecting the Game Center (iPhone) or Google Play Games (Android) account of a user who checked “I am 14 or older” to our servers.
  • Account save: a feature that saves your paws, purchases, dog list and game progress to your account so you can continue on a new phone. Photos are not saved.
  • Paws: the in-game currency. We keep paid paws (bought with money) separate from free paws (earned in the game).
  • Paid products: things you buy with money, such as the Full Version, extra dog slots, decorations sold for money (luxury harnesses, seasonal sets, premium collections), paw packs and custom dogs. Basic decorations are bought with paws.
  • Full Version: a bundle of paid features that you keep once bought. It includes 3 dog slots (including your first dog) and Full Version decorations, shows no banner or interstitial ads, and gives you rewards right away without watching an ad. Continuing a mini game (such as a revive in Hurdle Run) still means watching an ad, even with the Full Version.
  • Custom dog: a 3D dog we make individually from the photos and order form you send, and deliver to you.

3. Posting and changing these terms

These terms are posted on this website, and you can open them from the line at the bottom of the app’s Settings. We may change them within the law. We announce changes and the reasons 7 days before they take effect (30 days if unfavourable to users). If you do not agree with the changes, you may stop using the Service and delete your account.

4. Starting the Service and signing in

  • You can use the Service once you agree to these terms and the Privacy Policy when you first start.
  • “I am 14 or older” on the same screen is optional. If you check it, you can use account save and custom orders. If you do not, you can still play everything; your progress is stored only on your phone and nothing is sent to our account save or order servers. You can turn it on later in the app’s Settings.
  • There is no sign-in button. When you check “I am 14 or older”, the app connects automatically with Game Center on iPhone or Google Play Games on Android. If the connection does not happen or you decline it, you can still play.
  • With account save on, when you open the app on a new phone with the same Game Center or Google Play Games account, your paws, purchases, dog list and game progress come back. If the new phone already has other progress, a screen asks which one to use. Progress does not move between iPhone and Android.
  • Without account save, your progress is only on your phone, so you cannot continue if you change phones or delete the app. Even then, the Full Version and decorations bought with money come back with Restore purchases, and you can ask for a refund of unused paid paws under Section 8.
  • Custom orders need the “I am 14 or older” check and a connected account. Custom dogs you received can be downloaded again with the same account.
  • If a child under 14 checked the box, a legal guardian can tell us at hello@shallwedog.com and we will delete that account’s saved progress and account information (Privacy Policy, Section 3).

5. Providing and changing the Service

We aim to run the Service all year round. It may pause for maintenance, faults or network problems; when we know in advance, we announce it in the app. If a change to the Service makes a paid product you already bought unusable, we tell you in advance and compensate or refund you.

Without the Full Version, the app shows ads: banner ads at the bottom of the home and walk screens, interstitial ads right before the result screen of a walk, mini game, agility run or training (never before competitions or award ceremonies; at most once every 5 minutes, and none in the first 24 hours after you install), and ads you can choose to watch for a reward. The Full Version has no banner or interstitial ads, and rewards come right away without an ad. Continuing a mini game (such as a revive in Hurdle Run) still means watching an ad, even with the Full Version.

6. Ending the Service

  • We announce the end of the Service in the app and on the website 30 days in advance.
  • We refund unused paid paws.
  • After the Service ends, the app keeps working with on-device storage, and your on-device data and custom dogs you already received remain. Features that need a server (account save, downloading custom dogs again, new orders) stop working.

7. Paid products and payment

Paid products can be bought only through Apple App Store or Google Play billing. Prices and contents are shown on the product screen. Store payment and refund procedures also follow each store’s policies.

  • The Full Version and decorations bought with money (luxury harnesses, seasonal sets, premium collections) are yours to keep once bought, and come back with Restore purchases on the same store account, even on a new phone.
  • Premium collections and seasonal sets can be bought as a set or one item at a time.
  • Seasonal sets are in the shop during their season. When the season ends we take them out of the shop, and they may come back in the same season next year. Sets you already bought stay yours after the season ends.
  • Basic decorations are bought with paws. Paw packs and extra dog slots are new items each time you buy them.

8. Paws

  • We record paid paws (bought with money) and free paws (earned in the game) separately. With account save on, both are saved to your account separately.
  • Paid paws are valid for 5 years from the date of purchase. Free paws follow separately stated conditions.
  • When you spend paws, free paws (earned in the game) are used first, then paid paws.
  • You may withdraw from a purchase of unused paid paws within 7 days of buying them. If you used part of a pack, you may withdraw for the paid paws that are left.
  • After 7 days, we refund paid paws left within their validity period when you stop using the Service and delete your account and data. The amount is the price divided by the number of paws, minus a 10% fee. Please ask at hello@shallwedog.com (Help). The stores cannot refund part of a pack, so we send the money to your bank account ourselves. We ask only you for the account details by email and delete them right after sending.
  • Paws already spent and decorations bought with paws are not refunded.
  • If Apple or Google refunds a paw pack, we take back that many paid paws. If you have already spent them, your paw balance may go below zero; decorations bought with paws are not taken away.
  • Paws received as game rewards are free and cannot be refunded.
  • Paws cannot be transferred to others or exchanged for cash.

9. Withdrawal and refunds

  • You may withdraw from a purchase of a paid product within 7 days of the later of the purchase date and the date it became available.
  • However, under Article 17(2) of the Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce, withdrawal is limited for products that are used immediately and lose value, such as paws already spent, decorations already applied, or a Full Version or dog slot already opened and in use. We show this clearly and describe the product before payment; if we did not, you may withdraw.
  • Custom dogs are governed by Section 10.
  • If Apple or Google refunds a purchase, we take back that product. If a custom dog is refunded, the Full Version that came with it is also taken back.
  • Refunds are made through the store you paid with. Where we must refund you directly (including overpayments), we do so within 3 business days.

10. Custom dog orders

  • Order form: two photos (face from the front, whole body standing), breed (two for mixed breeds, or “not sure”), body type, current coat, features, one free-text line and your dog’s name.
  • We usually deliver to the app within 2–3 days, and at the latest within 7 days of payment. There is no preview.
  • Each photo must be under 5 MB, and the free-text line can be up to 60 characters.
  • We accept up to 3 custom orders per account per day (24 hours). A two-dog order counts as 2. Orders over the limit are not canceled; we continue making them the next day.
  • We start making your dog after the store confirms your payment. If confirmation is delayed, we check again every hour (for up to 48 hours); if it is still not confirmed, please email us.
  • A custom dog is made individually to order, so under Article 17(2)(6) of the Korean Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce and Article 21 of its Enforcement Decree, it cannot be withdrawn or refunded. We tell you this separately before payment and ask for your consent (a checkbox).
  • However, we refund you if we fail to deliver within 7 days or cannot make it for reasons on our side.
  • If you feel it does not look like your dog, instead of a refund we revise it once for free within 14 days of delivery.
  • A custom dog may differ from the real dog. We make it within what a 3D dog in the game can express.
  • Finished custom dogs are visible only in the ordering user’s account and are not shown to other users. You can download them again until you delete your account.
