How to get it

  1. Get Shall We Dog? on the App Store or Google Play.
  2. Log in, then enter the code in Settings › Invite friends › Enter a friend’s code.
  3. Finish your first walk and 🐾300 is yours.

You can enter a code once per account, within 7 days of creating your account. Each device can get it only once.

Your friend gets something too

Each time a friend you invited finishes their first walk, you get 🐾25×n for your nth friend (🐾25 for the first, 🐾50 for the second … up to 12 friends). At 3 friends you also get the Friend Bandana.
