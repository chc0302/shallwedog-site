Privacy Policy

Effective November 26, 2026 · 한국어

About this translation. The Korean version of this policy is the original. This English version is a reference translation. If the two differ, the Korean version applies, except where the law of your country requires otherwise.

K-Puff Lab (“we”, “us”) runs the Shall We Dog? app and the website shallwedog.com (the “Service”). This policy explains what personal information we process, why, for how long, and who helps us process it.

At a glance

  • Shall We Dog? is only for people aged 14 or older. When you first start, you must check “(Required) I am 14 or older” to begin. Your game progress is stored on your phone, and the app connects to our servers for account save (moving to a new phone) and custom orders.
  • There is no sign-in button. The connection is made automatically with Game Center on iPhone or Google Play Games on Android. If it does not happen, you can still play.
  • Photos sent for a Custom Dog order are deleted automatically 14 days after the last delivery. Photos from orders that were not paid for or whose order form was broken, so the dog was not made, are deleted after 7 days.
  • On the weekly leaderboard (Hurdle Run), other users see your dog’s name #tag, flag and score. Only while you yourself are in online features such as the plaza or matches, other users also see your dog’s look (including a Custom Dog) and the plaza character you chose. The real photos you sent are never shown.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we never delete your data on our own initiative.
  • You can delete your data at any time with Request data deletion at the bottom of the app’s Settings, or by email.

3. Only for ages 14 and older

Shall We Dog? is only for people aged 14 or older. When you first start, you must check “(Required) I am 14 or older” to begin (we do not ask for a date of birth). After you check it, the app connects to our servers for account save, custom orders, the weekly leaderboard and online features (the plaza, matches, etc.), and usage statistics (Firebase Analytics), crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics) and ad measurement (Meta) turn on. When the app starts, even before you check it, it downloads the shop list and item pictures from our server (Google Firebase). No personal information is sent for this; only the IP address needed for the download and a value that confirms the app is genuine go to Google.

If we learn that a child under 14 checked it untruthfully, or a legal guardian tells us at hello@shallwedog.com, we promptly delete that account’s saved progress, account information, orders and weekly leaderboard scores. Only the transaction records we must keep by law are kept separately as described in Section 2.

4. Sharing

We do not sell or give personal information to other companies, except when authorities request it through procedures set by law.

Some things are visible to other users inside the Service: your dog’s name #tag, flag and score on the weekly leaderboard, and your dog’s look (including a Custom Dog) and plaza character while you yourself are in online features (the plaza, matches, etc.). Real photos and order details are never shown.

6. Deletion

When a retention period ends or you ask us to delete, we delete within 5 days. Electronic files are deleted so they cannot be recovered; we do not keep personal information on paper. Information we must keep by law is stored separately and used only for that purpose. Copies in backups for recovery are deleted automatically after up to 30 days, as described in Section 2.

7. Your rights

  • You can ask to see, correct, delete or stop the processing of your information, and withdraw consent, at any time.
  • Tapping Request data deletion at the bottom of the app’s Settings deletes your server data (account information, account save records, Custom Dog re-downloads, order and production records, notification tokens, weekly leaderboard scores) and your server sign-in account (Firebase). The next time you open the app, it is disconnected from our servers and only the records on your phone remain. Paid paws left in your account save are deleted too, so you can ask for a refund first (Terms, Section 8); copies in backups for recovery are deleted after up to 30 days. Data on your device remains and is removed when you delete the app. See Account & data deletion.
  • Your weekly leaderboard scores are also deleted by a data deletion request (by email or on the Account & data deletion page) or by deleting your account. Once deleted, they leave the leaderboard right away. Your dog’s look and plaza character are visible only while you are in the plaza or a match, so if you do not want other users to see them, do not enter.
  • For other requests, email hello@shallwedog.com. After confirming your identity we reply within 10 days. A legal guardian or authorized representative may also make requests.
  • Transaction records we must keep by law (Section 2) are kept separately for that period even if you delete your account, and then deleted.

8. Advertising and analytics

To keep the game free, we show ads through Google LLC’s AdMob. Without the Full Version you see banner ads, interstitial ads after an activity ends and rewarded ads you choose to watch; with the Full Version there are only ads for continuing a mini game (Terms, Section 5). Google uses device information, in-app activity and an approximate location inferred from your IP address to show ads and measure them.

  • Personalized ads: we show ads based on your interests (on both Android and iPhone). On iPhone, only if you choose “Allow” when asked about tracking; in the EU and UK, only if you consent in the message below. You can turn personalized ads off in the app’s Settings.
  • Non-personalized ads: if you declined tracking or consent or turned personalized ads off, we ask Google to show only non-personalized ads.
  • iPhone: you can change tracking permission at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your iPhone.
  • EU and UK: before showing ads, we display a Google-certified consent message. If you do not consent, only non-personalized ads or limited ads that use less personal information are shown. You can change your choice in Settings at any time.
  • Android: you can delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads (menu names vary by device).

Ad measurement: to find out which ad led you to install the app, we use the Meta SDK from Meta Platforms, Inc. (for users in the EU, Meta Platforms Ireland Limited). It sends Meta in-app activity such as app launch, tutorial completion and purchases, device information and your advertising ID (Section 5). It is sent only after you check “I am 14 or older”, and where consent is required, such as in the EU and UK, only if you consent. If you do not want this, you can refuse as follows.

  • iPhone: if you do not choose “Allow” for tracking, your advertising identifier is not sent. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your iPhone.
  • Android: you can delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • In the app: if you withdraw your ad consent in the app’s Settings (turn personalized ads off, or in the EU and UK change your consent choice), nothing more is sent to Meta.

We use Google LLC’s Firebase for usage statistics and crash reports. We do not send names or contact details that identify you. Before you check “I am 14 or older”, no usage statistics or crash reports are sent (Section 3).

Website visit statistics: we use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits to shallwedog.com. It uses no cookies and only collects the page viewed, the referring page, browser type and country, which we look at only as totals. We never use it to identify who you are.

9. Notifications

  • Order notices (Custom Dog ready, new photos needed, refund) are sent to the person who ordered. We ask once, after you send your first custom order, whether you want them. Custom Dog ready notices are sent as soon as your dog is ready (this may be at night). New-photo requests and refund notices are also sent right away.
  • Play reminders are sent once a day at 8 pm, plus once at 6 pm if you have not given food or water that day. You get them only if you chose the evening reminder on the first start screen and allowed notifications on your phone; you can turn them on or off in the app’s Settings. They are scheduled on your phone and nothing is sent to our servers. They are never sent between 9 pm and 8 am.
  • Notifications never contain advertising such as discounts, prices or sales, so we do not ask for separate consent to marketing messages.
  • You can turn notifications off at any time in your phone’s notification settings.

10. Security

  • Only the operator can access personal information.
  • Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Server storage rules let each user read only their own data.
  • Work computers are password-locked, and service access keys are stored separately from code.
